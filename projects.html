Replicating "Lazy Prices"

Independent computational project · Jan–May 2025

What it does

This project replicates and extends a landmark Harvard "Lazy Prices" study using large-scale computational text analysis of three decades (1993–2024) of 10-K filings across S&P 500 firms. It combines text mining, vector similarity (cosine similarity), and financial econometrics to show that firms with more stable disclosure language sustained stronger long-term stock performance. I built the end-to-end Python pipeline spanning text processing, similarity scoring, and portfolio construction.

MABLE Indoor Navigation App

Capstone & Independent Study · with Dr. Vinod Namboodiri · Jan 2025 – May 2026

The problem

Indoor spaces — hospitals, campus buildings, transit hubs — have almost none of the wayfinding infrastructure that outdoor GPS relies on. For blind and low-vision users, that gap means every unfamiliar building is a navigation problem solved from scratch, usually by asking a stranger.

My process

I started with a competitive and landscape analysis of existing indoor navigation apps and smart-glasses technologies to see where the market gap actually was. From there, I mapped the end-to-end journey a blind or low-vision user takes through an unfamiliar building, to pin down exactly where friction happens:

That map made the real gap obvious: it isn't the entry or exit of a building, it's the middle, where indoor GPS fails and there's no tactile or audio replacement. From there, I authored technical specifications and a business case for accessibility-focused smart glasses, with UI/UX recommendations grounded directly in that friction point, and designed a user-testing protocol to validate usability and accessibility with target users ahead of implementation.

The outcome

A technical spec and business case for an indoor-navigation smart glasses product, plus a validated testing protocol ready to run with blind and low-vision users as the project moves into implementation.

Save Tuba — Sustainability Education App

Global Social Impact Fellowship · Lehigh University · Jan – Dec 2023

What it does

Save Tuba is a sustainability-education game. As a Global Social Impact Fellow, I led end-to-end qualitative research for the app, executing and synthesizing 6 focus groups with 30+ users across Kazakh- and Russian-medium schools during a field deployment in Kazakhstan. I translated that field research into new gamification and inquiry-based learning features for the product roadmap, and localized content and features for a multilingual, cross-cultural user base.

Semantic Search Tool for a Legal Case Archive

Data, Algorithms and Society Lab, Lehigh University · with Dr. Eric Baumer

What it does

Working with a repository of 3,800+ legal case files, I designed interview protocols and ran 40+ semi-structured interviews and think-aloud usability sessions with stakeholders at every level to understand their workflows and information needs. Those findings became UX specifications for search, filtering, navigation, and information architecture, which shipped as a semantic search tool integrated directly into the team's existing Dropbox workflow.
