Replicating "Lazy Prices"

Independent computational project · Jan–May 2025

What it does

This project replicates and extends a landmark Harvard "Lazy Prices" study using large-scale computational text analysis of three decades (1993–2024) of 10-K filings across S&P 500 firms. It combines text mining, vector similarity (cosine similarity), and financial econometrics to show that firms with more stable disclosure language sustained stronger long-term stock performance. I built the end-to-end Python pipeline spanning text processing, similarity scoring, and portfolio construction.

MABLE Indoor Navigation App

Capstone & Independent Study · with Dr. Vinod Namboodiri · Jan 2025 – May 2026

What it does

MABLE identifies a market opportunity in healthcare and accessibility through a competitive and landscape analysis of indoor navigation apps and smart-glasses technologies. I mapped user journeys and flows for blind and low-vision users to identify key friction points and accessibility gaps, then authored technical specifications and a business case for accessibility-focused smart glasses — with UI/UX recommendations grounded directly in that journey map. I also designed a user-testing protocol to validate usability and accessibility with target users ahead of implementation.

Save Tuba — Sustainability Education App

Global Social Impact Fellowship · Lehigh University · Jan – Dec 2023

What it does

Save Tuba is a sustainability-education game. As a Global Social Impact Fellow, I led end-to-end qualitative research for the app, executing and synthesizing 6 focus groups with 30+ users across Kazakh- and Russian-medium schools during a field deployment in Kazakhstan. I translated that field research into new gamification and inquiry-based learning features for the product roadmap, and localized content and features for a multilingual, cross-cultural user base.
